# Fashion Rules Knowledge Base

## Casual Occasions
- For casual outings, opt for comfortable, breathable fabrics like cotton or linen
- Layering is key for casual looks - combine basics with light jackets
- Neutral colors work well for everyday wear
- Comfortable shoes like sneakers or flats are ideal

## Formal Occasions
- Tailored pieces create a polished look
- Dark colors like navy, black, or charcoal are appropriate
- Crisp white shirts pair well with formal attire
- Closed-toe shoes are required for formal events

## Business Casual
- Blazers can elevate casual pieces
- Avoid overly casual items like jeans or sneakers
- Subtle patterns and textures add interest
- Comfortable yet professional shoes

## Summer Fashion
- Light, breathable fabrics prevent overheating
- Bright colors and patterns are perfect for summer
- Layering should be minimal in hot weather
- Sandals and open shoes are appropriate

## Winter Fashion
- Layering is essential for warmth
- Wool and cashmere provide excellent insulation
- Darker colors hide stains and wear better
- Boots and closed shoes protect from cold

## Color Coordination
- Monochromatic looks are always stylish
- Complementary colors create visual interest
- Black goes with everything
- White adds freshness to any outfit

## Body Type Considerations
- Vertical lines elongate the figure
- Well-fitted pieces flatter all body types
- Accessories can balance proportions
- Confidence in clothing enhances personal style

## Seasonal Trends
- Earth tones are timeless and versatile
- Classic pieces never go out of style
- Quality over quantity in wardrobe building
- Personal style should reflect individual preferences
